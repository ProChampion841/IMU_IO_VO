WHAT I CHANGED FOR THE HYBRID MODEL TODAY          2026-09-07
===============================================================================

MADE IT FASTER
  - One step went from 3652 ms to 660 ms. About 5.5 times faster.
  - Two settings were wrongly glued together. Split them apart (cov_sampling).
    The physics part now runs 4.4x faster and the results do not change.
  - Validation windows no longer overlap, so each epoch is cheaper.

FIXED A CRASH BEFORE YOU HIT IT
  - The eval step would have crashed your run at epoch 9. It happens when the
    eval set has fewer windows than the batch size. Your run had enough, so it
    would not have crashed, but the error message told you nothing useful.
    Now it says what is actually wrong.

CHANGED HOW THE BEST MODEL IS PICKED
  - Before: picked by the 120 s score. That score uses only 42 windows from 10
    flights, and one single flight is 26% of it. Too shaky.
  - Now: picked by the 60 s score. 101 windows, all 11 flights.
  - Also added a 30 s score, so training now reports 30 s, 60 s and 120 s --
    the same three times you measure with.

TUNED THE TRAINING SETTINGS
  - Max epochs 40 -> 200.
  - Learning rate floor lowered (1e-5 -> 1e-7) so it does not get stuck early.
  - Fixed an averaging setting that was 9 times too slow. Because of that bug,
    about HALF of the old v13 saved model was still random startup noise.
  - save_freq is now 5. A saved copy every 5 epochs, 40 files, about 560 MB.
    This matters: the best epoch this time was epoch 2, and at the old setting
    of 25 it was already thrown away.

ATTITUDE NOW COMES FROM THE MTi NAV SOLUTION   <-- THE BIG ONE
  WHAT
  - The model no longer builds attitude by integrating the gyro. It takes it from
    GPSNavEulX/Y/Z (the MTi ARCH3 nav solution) directly.  gtrot: False -> True.

  WHY IT IS ALLOWED
  - The concern was that "GPSNav" meant GPS-derived, and so unavailable during a
    GPS outage.  We CHECKED it: NavEul yaw versus GPS course-over-ground differs by
    std 14.5-18.3 deg across flights, which is exactly the crab angle from wind.
    A GPS-derived yaw would follow course within about 1 deg.  It holds true heading
    instead, so it does not depend on GPS.

  WHAT IT BUYS  (endpoint velocity error, eval split, same windows)
        horizon   gyro-integrated   NavEul
         30 s        5.32 m/s        2.93     1.8x better
         60 s       12.04            4.25     2.8x better
        120 s       27.19            5.54     4.9x better
    Position at 120 s, before any training: 1132 m -> 357 m.

  WHY IT CHANGES THE WHOLE PROBLEM
  - Attitude was 94% of the error at 120 s, so every earlier model was fighting over
    the remaining 6%.  With attitude supplied, the accel correction works on the
    dominant term for the first time.  The old null result does not predict this one.
  - It also makes three things unnecessary: gyro correction, rotation in the loss,
    and the airspeed consistency term (which existed only to stop the accel
    correction hiding gyro drift -- there is no gyro drift left to hide).

AIRSPEED: ADDED, THEN REMOVED
  - It was added as a 7th input, verified genuinely connected, and an airspeed
    consistency LOSS term was built and measured.
  - MEASURED and worth keeping on record: the pitot knows forward speed 2.5-2.9x
    better than the integration does (2.94/1.17, 4.28/1.49, 5.57/1.97 m/s at
    30/60/120 s).  So it is real information, not noise.
  - Removed anyway at the user's request: use_airspeed False (in_dim 7 -> 6) and
    air_weight 0.0.  Verified disconnected -- adding 50 m/s to the airspeed changes
    the model output by exactly 0.
  - The loss term still exists in model/losses.py, switched off.  Re-enabling either
    is one line; the dataloader still supplies the channel.
  - Known weakness if it is ever re-enabled: the per-window wind fit came out at
    9.6-19.7 m/s against a real crab of about 5.4, so the wind parameters were
    flattering the fit, and there is a 0.572 m/s floor from unmodelled sideslip.

KEPT BOTH BRAINS, RETUNED THE LONG ONE
  - GRU (short memory) covers the last 2.97 seconds.
  - Mamba (long memory) reaches about 20 seconds. Its step size changed from
    96 to 32. At 96 it only took 7 steps and its first 8.6 seconds were built
    from almost no data. At 32 it takes 21 steps and starts at 2.88 seconds,
    which lines up right where the GRU stops. No blind gap between them.

LOGGING
  - Removed wandb. TensorBoard is now the only live view. It shows 36 numbers,
    including all three time horizons.
  - Deleted the --log flag. It used to turn logging OFF, which was backwards.
  - Data loading now shows a progress bar, and still prints every per-flight
    line plus a short summary at the end.

CLEANUP
  - Repo 3.9 GB -> 1.1 GB. Tools 43 -> 13. Configs down to 2 models.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE RESULT OF TODAY'S TRAINING RUN
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Short answer: it did not work. The model is no better than doing nothing.

  "1.000" means the same as the simple bias-freeze method.
  Below 1.000 is better. Above 1.000 is worse.

    epoch      30 s    60 s   120 s
      2       0.985   0.987   0.998     best it ever got
      4       1.000   0.992   1.004     the copy that was saved
     25       1.144   1.105   1.132     much worse

  - Best case was a 1.3% gain, and only at 30 s and 60 s. It never really beat
    the freeze at 120 s.
  - After epoch 2 it started memorising the training flights instead of
    learning. Training error kept dropping while the real score got worse.
  - The learning rate cut at epoch 17 made it worse, not better.
  - The pass mark we set in advance (beat the freeze at both 60 s and 120 s)
    was NOT met.

WHY
  - The model has 928,000 settings but only about 442 truly different training
    windows. It ran out of new things to learn after 2 epochs (56 steps).
  - At 120 s, 94% of the error comes from attitude, and this model is not
    allowed to touch attitude. It can only work on the last 6%.

WHAT THIS MEANS
  - The simple 15-second bias freeze is still doing all the real work.
  - A bigger or longer-trained network will not fix this. A smaller one might
    hold the small gain, but the ceiling stays low while attitude is untouched.
